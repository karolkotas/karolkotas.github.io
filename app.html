FAQ

Good to know.

Quick answers about downloads, offline listening and privacy. Still stuck? Email us.

Is Mp3 Music Downloader & Player free?

Yes. The app is free to download and use, and is supported by ads.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, login or profile. Open the app, search and start listening.

Can I listen without internet?

Yes. Tracks you download are saved on your device and can be played offline.

Where are downloads saved?

Downloads are saved to the Music folder on your device using Android's Download Manager. If that folder isn't available, the app falls back to Downloads.

Which Android versions are supported?

Mp3 Music Downloader & Player supports Android 7.0 (API 24) and newer.

How do I remove a download?

Open your device's Files or Downloads app, go to the Music folder and delete the MP3 files you no longer need.

What data does the app collect?

The app does not require an account or profile. Third-party advertising and analytics providers may collect device, advertising and usage information as described in the privacy policy.

Why do I see ads?

Ads keep the app free. They are mediated by TopOn, which works with multiple advertising networks. See the privacy policy for details and opt-out information.
